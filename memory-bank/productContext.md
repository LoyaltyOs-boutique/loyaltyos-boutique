# Product Context — LoyaltyOS

## Why This Product Exists

85 Lansdowne (85B, Sarat Bose Road, Kolkata) is a multi-designer luxury fashion boutique. Loyalty is currently run on paper cards and ad-hoc WhatsApp blasts — the owner cannot see points, tiers, or repeat-purchase behaviour centrally, and client relationships depend on memory rather than a system.

## Problems It Solves

1. **Paper loyalty cards are untracked** — no central view of points, tiers or purchase history.
2. **Ad-hoc WhatsApp blasts** — no segmentation, no reach estimation, no delivery stats.
3. **Confidential styling records are scattered** — measurements and private staff notes need a permissioned home (merchant-only).
4. **POS and loyalty are disconnected** — billing, points accrual and redemption risk divergence (double/lost points).
5. **No client self-service** — customers have no portal for points, lookbooks, styling record or reviews.

## User Personas

| Persona | Description | Primary Needs |
|---------|-------------|---------------|
| Store Owner / Merchant (`Arpita` in demo) | Runs the boutique; enters via login | Full dashboard, billing, campaigns, customer insights |
| Store Staff (shared merchant account) | Handles billing counter, client data entry | Fast client lookup, billing, quick access to notes |
| Client / Customer (`Ananya Mukherjee`) | Shopper with a loyalty account | Points balance, lookbooks, measurements access, reviews |
| Designer Brand Partners | Seesa, SVA, Koakh, House of Ara, Ridhi Mehra | Their lookbooks featured; no direct login in v1 |

## Core User Journey

1. **Merchant logs in** → Dashboard shows metrics, delight queue, recent orders, tier distribution.
2. **Billing** → Look up client by phone → bill items → apply points discount → complete → points accrue atomically. 
3. **CRM** → Search/filter clients → view/add confidential measurements + staff notes → purchase history.
4. **Campaigns** → Compose WhatsApp broadcast with segmentation (tier/points/tags) → estimated reach → send.
5. **Customer portal** → Login via OTP → balance + tier progress, lookbooks, styling record, reviews (+150), GMB (+300), testimonial CTA (₹500 credit).
